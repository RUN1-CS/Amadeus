# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Amadeus project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or technical ability.

We value constructive collaboration, clear communication, and respect for the time and effort contributors put into the project.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Discussing technical disagreements in good faith.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people.
- Respecting different levels of experience and allowing room for questions.
- Sharing relevant context when reporting problems or proposing changes.
- Protecting other people's privacy and not publishing private information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith arguments.
- Insulting or humiliating contributors.
- Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation hostile or unsafe.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other channels used for Amadeus project work. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately through the contact method listed in the repository profile or project documentation.

Please include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Do not use public issues to report sensitive incidents.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. A request to stop the behavior.
2. A warning.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal of content or contributions.
5. A temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

The response will depend on the severity and context of the behavior. Maintainers will aim to be fair, consistent, and proportionate.
